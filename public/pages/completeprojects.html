Human Computer Interaction Projects

  • The Skribbler (2026)
    • This was a prototype I made for a class at UChicago, and the idea behind the project was to turn an everyday tool (in this case, a pen) into a tool for art or creativity.
    • Scribbles and draws on its own, run by a servo motor and powered by an Arduino board.
    • Scribble-arc controlled using a potentiometer
    • Find out more about the process of making this prototype as well as other photos of the mechanism at this link.
  • Socket Buddy (2026)
